The goal of this training env is to train the G1 to pick up the cube with its right hand and bring it to a fixed hold point in front of its chest. The G1 stands
fixed base at the table with its left arm down by its side and its right hand over the table. The cube spawns at the table center. Reaching the hold point ends
the episode successfully. No domain randomization.

Reward Terms (cfg = g1_pick_cube_env_cfg.py RewardsCfg, fn = mdp/functions.py):
    reaching        palm toward the cube                              cfg L235  mdp.palm_to_object_distance_reward fn L120
    grasp_fingers   close the fingers when the palm is at the cube    cfg L246  mdp.fingers_closed_near_object fn L134
    lifting         raise the cube from rest toward lift height       cfg L255  mdp.object_lift_progress fn L158
    goal_tracking   carry the cube toward the chest hold point        cfg L260  mdp.object_to_target_distance_reward fn L170
    success_bonus   one-time bonus when the cube reaches the target   cfg L267  mdp.is_terminated_term
    dropped_penalty one-time penalty for losing the cube              cfg L268  mdp.is_terminated_term
    action_l2       small smoothness penalty                          cfg L269  mdp.action_l2_clamped fn L106
    action_rate_l2  small smoothness penalty                          cfg L270  mdp.action_rate_l2_clamped fn L111

Termination Conditions (cfg = g1_pick_cube_env_cfg.py TerminationsCfg, fn = mdp/functions.py):

    Cube lost                     cube_dropped   cfg L286  mdp.object_out_of_bound fn L201 (off the table or out of reach)
    Physics blow-up               robot_exploded cfg L300  mdp.robot_or_object_state_invalid fn L216 (joint vel over 2x limit)
    Timeout                       time_out       cfg L277  (3 s episode)
